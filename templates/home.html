Secure Login System

Welcome to the Secure Login System

This is a simple login system that uses bcrypt for password hashing and salting. The system is created by
Alakhe Ngalo. It is designed to provide a secure way for users to register and log in to their accounts,
while also ensuring that if their passwords would accidentally be leaked, they would be protected by the hashing and salting process.
None of the data that will be stored will contain any sensitive information about the users.

What will happen to the passwords?

After users register, the application analyzes the strength of their chosen password by comparing it against a dictionary of common passwords.
Passwords that are recovered demonstrate why predictable passwords are insecure

What is a Dictionary Attack?

A dictionary attack is a cyberattack where hackers use automated software to crack passwords by systematically testing a large, predefined
list of likely words and phrases. Because people often use simple, predictable passwords, these attacks are much faster and more efficient
than guessing random character combinations.

Why do we need strong passwords?

Strong passwords are essential for protecting sensitive information and preventing unauthorized access to accounts.
They help safeguard personal data, financial information, and online identities from cyber threats.
A strong password typically includes a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, making it difficult for attackers to guess or crack.
By using strong passwords, individuals can significantly reduce the risk of identity theft, data breaches, and other security incidents.

Tips for Creating Strong Passwords:

  • Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
  • Avoid using easily guessable information such as names, birthdays, or common words.
  • Make your password at least 12 characters long.
  • Consider using a passphrase made up of random words or a sentence that is easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords securely.
